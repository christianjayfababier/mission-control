    Repository & GitHub account

    The account's token and identity are set in the environment of every new terminal for this project (GH_TOKEN, GIT_AUTHOR_*/GIT_COMMITTER_*), so gh and git push act as that account while other projects keep theirs. Existing terminals keep their old identity: close and reopen them.

    Team & models

    Each role runs at a model and effort. Custom roles come from the project's .claude/agents files and are saved back there; built-in types are stored by Mission Control and handed to the lead. Leave a field empty to let the lead decide. The lead may still escalate a stuck worker to Fable.
